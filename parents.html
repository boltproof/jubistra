What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a block-based building and exploration game made by Mojang Studios (owned by Microsoft). Players explore a world made of blocks, build structures, and interact with the environment. It's rated E for Everyone (ESRB) and PEGI 7+ in Europe.

Minecraft has two main editions:

  • Bedrock Edition — works on Windows, Mac, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, iOS, and Android. Uses .mcworld files.
  • Java Edition — works on Windows, Mac, and Linux. Uses .zip files with schematic data.

You can buy Minecraft from minecraft.net. We don't sell it or make money from it — we just build worlds that run inside it.

Age recommendations

  • Ages 3-6: Explore the world together with a parent. The builds are visual and peaceful — great for shared exploration.
  • Ages 7-12: Explore independently. Voting is allowed with a parent's help. Great for school projects about UK & Ireland geography.
  • Ages 13+: Full access including our Discord community (13+, Discord's minimum age). Can suggest towns, share builds, and help with community events.

What to talk about with your child

  • Geography: Where is this town? How far is it from where you live? What country/region is it in?
  • History: What historical events happened here? Why is the town famous?
  • Architecture: What kinds of buildings does the town have? How are they different from where you live?
  • Scale and mapping: Compare the Minecraft version to a satellite map. What did the builders choose to include? What did they leave out?
  • Community: Which town would you like to see next? Vote together!

Screen time and balance

Minecraft is creative and open-ended — but like all screen time, it's best in moderation. We recommend:

  • Set a timer before starting (20-40 minutes is plenty for exploring one town).
  • Take breaks every 20 minutes (the 20-20-20 rule: look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds).
  • Talk about what you saw after the session — the educational value comes from the conversation, not just the game.

If something goes wrong

If you find content that's inappropriate, broken, or concerning:

  • Email safety@jubistra.com — we respond within 24 hours for child safety concerns.
  • Stop using the world immediately if it contains anything problematic.
  • Delete the world from your device: in Minecraft, go to World List → select the world → Delete.
