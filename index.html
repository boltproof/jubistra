⸱ For Parents

Safety first — always

Jubistra is built for families. Here's what that means in practice.

  • ✓Worlds included in your subscription. Download .mcworld files for any build — no extra charges per world.
  • ✓No chat, no multiplayer, no in-game communication. Our worlds are single-player downloads. What your child explores stays on their device.
  • ✓No ads, no tracking, no microtransactions. One subscription covers everything — no upsells, no hidden charges.
  • ✓No personal data collected from under-13s. Voting requires no name or email — just a click. Under-13s should vote with a parent's help.
  • ✓Content is family-friendly. All builds are of real towns — no violence, no horror, no mature themes.
  • ✓Open source builds. World files and build schematics are shared openly so anyone can learn, modify, or verify them.
  • ✓Community moderation. Our Discord (13+) is moderated by trained staff with clear community guidelines.
  • ✓GDPR & COPPA compliant. Full privacy policy and cookie policy available — no hidden tracking, ever.

⸱ FAQ

Frequently asked

Is Jubistra free? +
Jubistra is €5/month with a 7-day free trial. Voting is free for everyone — no account needed to vote. Downloading worlds requires a subscription. No ads, no microtransactions, no premium tier. Cancel anytime.
Do I need Minecraft to play? +
Yes — you need a copy of Minecraft (Bedrock Edition for .mcworld files, or Java Edition for .zip files). Minecraft is sold separately by Mojang/Microsoft. We don't sell it or make money from it.
What scale are the towns built at? +
Most towns are built at roughly 1:2 or 1:3 scale — enough to feel like the real place while staying buildable in a week. A 5,000-person town might be 400–600 blocks per side. We prioritise recognisable landmarks and street layouts over exact 1:1 accuracy.
How do the five streams work? +
Each country — Ireland, Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales, and England — has its own weekly poll with 10 small towns. Polls open Monday 9am and close Saturday 9pm. You can vote in every stream (one vote per stream). The winner of each stream gets built when its turn comes up in the rotation.
Is this safe for my child? +
Yes. Our worlds contain no violence, chat, multiplayer, or mature content. Downloads don't require an account or personal data. If your child is under 13, please help them vote and download. See our Parent's Guide for full details.
Can I suggest a town? +
Absolutely — join our Discord (13+) or email hello@jubistra.com with your suggestion. Include the town name, county, and why it's special. We add the most-nominated towns to the next poll.
Who makes Jubistra? +
Jubistra is made by a small team of Minecraft builders and parents based in Ireland and the UK. We're not affiliated with Mojang, Microsoft, or any game company. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang Studios.
Why five separate polls? +
Each country — Ireland, Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales, and England — has its own weekly poll so every nation gets regular builds. You can vote in all five streams (one vote per stream). The winner of each stream gets built when its turn comes up in the rotation.
