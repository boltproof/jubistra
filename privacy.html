Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

🛡️ The short version

We collect the minimum data needed to run a subscription: your email (for billing), your child's Minecraft gamertag (for the server whitelist), and your country (for voting). No tracking, no ads, no selling data. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and your rights.

👶 Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K, UK Age Appropriate Design Code)

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. When you subscribe, we collect the parent/guardian's email (for billing) and your child's Minecraft gamertag (for the server whitelist). The gamertag is not personal data — it's a public in-game name chosen by the player.

Voting requires only a first name (which can be a nickname) and a country. No email, no account, no profile.

If a parent believes their child has provided personal data (e.g. a real name or address in a suggestion email), email safety@jubistra.com and we will delete it immediately.

Discord (13+): our Discord server follows Discord's minimum age of 13. We do not knowingly allow under-13s. Discord's own privacy practices apply there — see Discord's Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Jubistra ("we", "us") is a non-commercial project operating jubistra.com, minngland.com, minreland.com, minscotland.com, and minwales.com. We are based in Ireland and the United Kingdom. We are not a company — we do not sell anything or generate revenue.

2. What data we collect

Almost none. Here's the complete list:

  • Subscription data: When you subscribe, your email address is stored by our payment processor (Ko-fi for Card/PayPal, or BTCPay Server for Bitcoin). We use your email to send subscription confirmations, server whitelist instructions, and renewal reminders. We do not use it for marketing or sell it to anyone.
  • Minecraft gamertag: When you subscribe, you email us your child's Minecraft Bedrock gamertag so we can add it to the server whitelist. This is a public in-game name, not personal data. We store it only to manage the whitelist and remove it when you cancel.
  • Voting data: When you vote in a weekly poll, we store a first name (or nickname) and the country you're voting from. That's it. No email, no IP address, no account, no cookie set by the vote itself.
  • Email correspondence: If you email hello@jubistra.com or safety@jubistra.com, we see your email address and your message. We don't add you to a mailing list (we don't have one). We delete emails after the conversation is resolved.
  • Anonymous analytics: We may use privacy-respecting analytics (e.g. self-hosted Plausible or similar) that show aggregate page views without tracking individuals. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no fingerprinting. See our Cookie Policy for details.
  • Hosting logs: Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps standard server logs for security and uptime. We do not access or use these for profiling.

3. What we DON'T collect

  • No accounts or passwords
  • No email addresses (unless you email us)
  • No phone numbers
  • No home addresses
  • No payment information — we never see your card details (Ko-fi/PayPal/Stripe handle that). Bitcoin payments are received via BTCPay Server — we see a transaction hash, not your identity.
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No cross-site tracking
  • No data from children under 13 beyond a first name and country

4. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Ask what data we hold about you (almost nothing).
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: Ask us to delete your data. Email hello@jubistra.com and we will delete it.
  • Object: Object to processing. Since we don't process data for advertising or profiling, this mostly means deleting your vote or email.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format (it'll be a very short file).
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — the DPC (Ireland) or ICO (UK).

To exercise any right, email hello@jubistra.com. We respond within 30 days (usually within 48 hours).

5. How long we keep data

  • Vote data: Deleted after each weekly poll closes and the winning town is announced. Typically 7-10 days.
  • Email correspondence: Deleted 90 days after the conversation is resolved.
  • Analytics: Aggregate data kept for up to 12 months, then aggregated further or deleted.

6. Who we share data with

Almost no one. We don't sell, rent, or share data with third parties for commercial purposes — because we're not commercial. The only third parties who process any data are:

  • Cloudflare — our hosting/DNS provider. They process IP addresses for routing and security. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — loaded via Google Fonts CDN for website typography. Google may see your IP address when your browser requests the font file. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Discord — if you join our Discord (13+), Discord's privacy policy applies. See Discord's Privacy Policy.

We do not share data with advertisers (we have none), data brokers, social media platforms, or anyone else.

7. International transfers

Our servers are in the EU (Cloudflare) and our team is in Ireland and the UK. If you access the site from outside the EU/UK, your data may be processed in your region. We do not transfer EU/UK personal data to countries without adequate data protection safeguards.

8. Security

We use HTTPS encryption (TLS) for all pages. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) provides DDoS protection and rate limiting. We do not store passwords (no accounts). We do not store payment data (everything is free). If a breach occurs, we will notify affected users within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

9. Children's data — extra protections

We follow the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code) and GDPR Article 8:

  • No profiling of children
  • No nudge techniques or dark patterns aimed at children
  • No geolocation tracking
  • No behavioural advertising
  • Default settings are maximally private
  • Voting under 13 requires parental involvement (we ask parents to help)
  • Discord access is 13+ only (Discord's minimum age)

10. Contact

Email hello@jubistra.com for privacy questions, or safety@jubistra.com for child safety concerns. We respond within 48 hours for privacy and within 24 hours for child safety.
