By subscribing to Jubistra, you agree to pay a recurring monthly fee:

  • Card / PayPal: €5.00/month, billed monthly via Ko-fi/PayPal.
  • Bitcoin: €4.50/month (10% discount), billed monthly via BTCPay Server.

Free trial: Your first 7 days are free. You can cancel at any time during the trial and you will not be charged. After day 7, the monthly fee is automatically charged to your payment method.

Cancellation: You can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no cancellation fees and no minimum contract. Cancel via the Ko-fi portal (Card/PayPal), the BTCPay dashboard (Bitcoin), or email hello@jubistra.com.

Refunds: Due to the nature of digital content, monthly subscription fees are non-refundable once charged. However, if you cancel during the 7-day free trial, you will not be charged at all. If you believe you were charged in error, email hello@jubistra.com within 14 days and we will review it.

3. What you can do with our worlds

  • Download and play — all worlds are available to subscribers as single-player downloads.
  • Join the shared server — your Minecraft Bedrock gamertag is whitelisted after you subscribe.
  • Share with family — members of your household may use your subscription. Please don't share your login with people outside your household.
  • Modify and build on — add your own buildings, change the terrain, make it yours.
  • Use in educational settings — teachers and parents may use our worlds in classrooms, homeschooling, or community groups (one subscription per class/family).
  • Record and stream — make videos or screenshots of our worlds. We just ask you credit Jubistra and link to our site.

4. What you can't do

  • Sell our worlds — you may not charge money for access to them, bundle them into a paid product, or put them behind a paywall.
  • Share your subscription — your subscription is for your household. Sharing login credentials or server whitelist entries with people outside your household is not permitted.
  • Claim you made them — credit Jubistra when sharing or streaming.
  • Use them for commercial purposes — no advertising, no sponsored content, no brand partnerships inside or alongside our worlds without written permission.
  • Use the shared server to harass others — our server is family-friendly. Any harassment, griefing, or inappropriate behaviour will result in removal from the whitelist without refund.

5. The shared Minecraft server

Subscribers get access to a shared Minecraft Bedrock server. Rules:

  • Whitelist only: Only gamertags added after subscription can join. Email hello@jubistra.com with your child's gamertag to be added.
  • Chat is disabled: The server has in-game chat turned off. No text or voice communication between players.
  • No griefing: Don't destroy or modify other players' builds. The server is for exploring our towns together, not for combat or destruction.
  • Family-friendly: No inappropriate builds, signs, or behaviour. Violations result in whitelist removal without refund.
  • Server uptime: We aim for 99% uptime but don't guarantee it. The server may be down for maintenance, updates, or technical issues. We do not offer refunds for server downtime.

6. Voting and community

Weekly polls let our subscribers choose which town gets built next. Rules:

  • One vote per subscriber per poll.
  • Voters must be 7 or older. Voters under 13 should vote with a parent or guardian's help.
  • We reserve the right to remove nominations that are inappropriate, duplicative, or outside the UK and Ireland.
