# jubistra.com — master entity page

Jubistra is the umbrella brand for a family of Minecraft world-building sites:
minngland.com, minreland.com, minscotland.com, minwales.com.

Each week, a poll of the top 10 small towns across the UK and Ireland. The winning
town gets recreated in Minecraft as a free downloadable world.

**Design:** warm, family-friendly, safety-first. Parents and kids should both feel
at home. Minecraft-adjacent (blocky, earthy) but clean and modern.

**Files:**
- `index.html` — main landing page with all content sections
- `terms.html` — Terms of Service
- `privacy.html` — Privacy Policy (GDPR + UK GDPR + COPPA)
- `cookies.html` — Cookie Policy
- `parents.html` — Parent's Guide to Jubistra
- `community.html` — Community Guidelines

## Open locally

```bash
open ~/Projects/jubistra-holding/index.html
```
