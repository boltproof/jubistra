Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

🛡️ The short version

We don't use tracking cookies. We don't use advertising cookies. We don't use social media cookies. The only cookies we might use are for essential site functions (if we add a voting system that needs one) and privacy-respecting analytics that don't track individuals.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser when you visit a website. Some are essential (e.g. remembering that you've already voted), some are for analytics (counting page views), and some are for tracking and advertising (which we do not use).

2. Cookies we use

If and when we add features that need cookies, here's what we'll use:

  • Essential cookies — if we add a voting system, we may set a cookie to prevent double-voting. This cookie stores no personal data — just a random ID that expires after the poll closes (7-10 days).
  • Analytics (optional) — if we add privacy-respecting analytics (e.g. self-hosted Plausible), it uses a single first-party cookie to count unique visitors without identifying them. No cross-site tracking, no advertising, no profiling.

3. Cookies we DON'T use

  • No advertising cookies (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • No social media cookies (Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram, etc.)
  • No cross-site tracking cookies
  • No fingerprinting
  • No remarketing or retargeting
  • No third-party analytics (Google Analytics, etc.)

4. Third-party cookies

When you load our website, your browser may contact these third parties:

  • Google Fonts — loads our web fonts. Google may set cookies via their fonts API. See Google's cookie policy. You can block this in your browser settings without affecting our site.
  • Cloudflare — our hosting/DNS provider. Cloudflare may set a essential cookie (__cf_bm) for bot protection. This does not track you across sites. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

These third parties set their own cookies that we do not control. Your browser's cookie settings can block them.

5. How to control cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and data stored

Blocking all cookies won't break our site — it's mostly static. You might just be able to vote twice (we trust you not to!).

6. Children and cookies

We do not set cookies that collect data from children. The only cookie a child might receive is the essential vote-prevention cookie (a random ID with no personal data). We do not use cookies to profile, track, or advertise to children.

7. Contact

Email hello@jubistra.com with cookie questions. See our Privacy Policy for full data practices.
