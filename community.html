Community Guidelines

Last updated: 3 September 2026

🛡️ The short version

Be kind. Be safe. Respect each other. Don't ruin it for everyone. That's most of it. Read on for the details.

1. Who these rules apply to

These guidelines apply to Jubistra's community spaces — our Discord server (13+), social media, and any other community platforms we operate. Downloading worlds from our website doesn't require following these (no account needed), but if you join our community, please read and follow them.

2. Our golden rules

✅ Be kind

Treat everyone with respect. Disagree about towns, not about people. No name-calling, bullying, or harassment — ever.

✅ Be welcoming

Everyone is welcome here regardless of age, background, where they're from, or how good at Minecraft they are. We especially welcome kids and families — act accordingly.

✅ Be honest

Don't cheat on votes (one per person). Don't pretend to be someone you're not. Don't claim other people's builds as your own.

✅ Be helpful

If someone's new to Minecraft or Jubistra, help them out. Share your builds. Suggest towns. Make the community better than you found it.

✅ Stay on topic

We're here to talk about Minecraft towns, UK & Ireland geography, and building. Keep discussions relevant. Off-topic is fine in small doses; derailing every thread is not.

3. What gets you removed

🚫 Bullying, harassment, or hate speech

Targeted attacks, slurs, hate speech, or harassment of any kind. Instant ban, no warnings.

🚫 Sharing personal information

Don't share your own or anyone else's real name, address, phone number, school, or location — especially if you're under 18. Use a nickname.

🚫 Inappropriate content

No NSFW, violence, gore, or mature content. Our community is family-friendly. This includes builds, screenshots, links, and text.

🚫 Spam or scams

No spam, no advertising, no phishing links, no "free Minecraft accounts" (they're scams), no self-promotion without permission.

🚫 Vote manipulation

One vote per person per poll. Creating multiple accounts to vote is not allowed — and since we don't even require accounts, there's no excuse!

🚫 Impersonation

Don't pretend to be a Jubistra team member, a Mojang employee, or anyone you're not.

4. Moderation

Our Discord and community spaces are moderated by trained volunteer staff. Moderators can:

  • Warn users who break rules
  • Delete rule-breaking messages
  • Temporarily mute or suspend users
  • Permanently ban users for serious or repeated violations

Moderators' decisions are final but can be appealed by emailing hello@jubistra.com. Appeals are reviewed within 48 hours.

5. Children in our community

Our Discord server requires users to be 13+ (Discord's minimum age). We do not knowingly allow under-13s to join Discord. Our website voting is open to all ages (with parental help for under-13s), but our Discord community is strictly 13+.

If you're under 18 using our Discord:

  • Use a nickname, not your real name
  • Don't share your age, school, or location
  • Don't share photos of yourself or your home
  • If someone makes you uncomfortable, message a moderator immediately

6. Reporting

If you see someone breaking these rules or feel unsafe:

  • In Discord: message any moderator (look for the role badge) or use the report function
  • By email: safety@jubistra.com — we respond within 24 hours for safety concerns
  • For urgent child safety: email safety@jubistra.com with "URGENT" in the subject line

7. What happens when you're reported

When a report is filed, a moderator reviews it within 24 hours. Depending on severity:

  • Minor (first offence): Warning via DM, message deleted.
  • Repeated minor: 24-hour mute.
  • Major (harassment, NSFW, predatory behaviour): Permanent ban, reported to Discord Trust & Safety if applicable.
  • Child safety: Immediate ban, report to relevant authorities, 24-hour response to the reporter.

8. Changes

We may update these guidelines as our community grows. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Major changes will be announced in Discord.

9. Credit

These guidelines were inspired by community standards from Internet Matters and Childnet. We're grateful for their work in keeping kids safe online.
